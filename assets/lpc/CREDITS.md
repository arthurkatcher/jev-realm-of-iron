# LPC character sprite credits

Sheets in this folder were composited with Pillow from layers of the
[Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/sanderfrenken/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
(credit records taken from its `sheet_definitions/*.json`; `CREDITS.csv` used as fallback).
Every layer is offered under one or more of CC-BY 3.0/4.0, CC-BY-SA 3.0/4.0, OGA-BY 3.0, GPL 3.0 or CC0.
Where a layer is multi-licensed we use it under the licence heading it is listed beneath; all offered licences are shown per entry.

Because several layers are CC-BY-SA 3.0 only, each composited sheet as a whole must be distributed under **CC-BY-SA 3.0** (or GPL 3.0 where every layer offers it). Licence text: `cc-by-sa-3_0.txt` (copied from the repo). OGA-BY 3.0 text: https://static.opengameart.org/OGA-BY-3.0.txt (not shipped in the repo).
